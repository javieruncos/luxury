## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-end architectural curation, exclusive real estate development, and prime residential brokerage. It merges the gravitas of quiet luxury with the razor-sharp precision of contemporary modernist architecture. 

The aesthetic sits at the intersection of high-fashion editorial publishing and refined functional modernism:
- **Cinematic & Atmospheric:** Photography dominates the sensory experience, grounded by architectural dusk/twilight perspectives, moody depth, and organic masonry.
- **High-Impact Contrast:** Grounded charcoal neutrals and stark white canvases are punctuated by a striking, hyper-modern chartreuse/lime accent tone that commands attention for conversion points.
- **Sophisticated Asymmetry:** Compositions reject rigid corporate templates in favor of asymmetric grid layouts, generous negative space, floating frosted pills, and micro-cards that evoke bespoke portfolio curation.
- **Materiality & Restraint:** Physical weight is implied through clean glassmorphic floating bars, soft structural radii, hairline borders, and tight typographic tracking.

## Colors

The palette pairs high-luxury monochrome foundations with an energetic, hyper-modern lime highlight. 

### Roles & Semantics
- **Primary (`#D8F235`):** High-voltage chartreuse/lime. Used strictly for high-priority interactive calls to action, active navigation badges, and critical visual hooks. Typography set on this background is always deep carbon black (`#111111`).
- **Secondary (`#111111` / `#1A1A1A`):** Deep architectural carbon and obsidian. Serves as the primary typography color on light backdrops, hero pill containers, and secondary interactive buttons.
- **Tertiary (`#666666` / `#777777`):** Neutral slate grey for metadata, secondary descriptions, rating denominator labels, and muted lead-ins.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FFFFFF` & `#F8F9FA`):** Crisp pure white paired with subtle off-white parchment for structural section breaks, preventing sterile starkness while keeping photography front and center.
- **Glass & Overlays:** Translucent black tiers (`rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.65)` to `rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.85)`) coupled with 1px hairline perimeter borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)` on dark scenes; `rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.08)` on light surfaces).

## Typography

The typographic signature is geometric, architectural, and razor-sharp. **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides clean modern neo-grotesque authority with refined geometric terminals.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Execution
- **Tight Display Tracking:** All headlines and hero statements utilize negative letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.035em`) to evoke Swiss modernist poster discipline and high-end editorial magazines.
- **Metric Pairing:** Numerical statistics (e.g., `"4.9"`) leverage massive display scaling with tight baseline-aligned denominators (`"/5.0"`) in secondary grey weights.
- **Editorial Sub-copy:** Body paragraphs remain light and airy with comfortable leading (`1.6x` to `1.75x`), providing high legibility against expansive white or moody charcoal backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy balances asymmetrical art direction with a systematic 12-column fluid grid.

### Layout Grid Rules
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fixed outer boundaries up to `1440px` with a fluid fallback. Margins of `3.5rem` (`56px`) with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters. Heavy use of asymmetrical column balancing (e.g., left-heavy 7-column headline paired with an offset 4-column descriptive body).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** `2rem` (`32px`) margins and `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters. Floating preview cards scroll horizontally off-canvas.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters. Stacks components vertically while maintaining prominent pill-shaped CTAs.

### Spacing Rhythm
Sectional vertical rhythm is expansive. Key sections maintain `5rem` to `7rem` between narrative chapters to reinforce grandeur and breathing room, while component interiors utilize tight `0.75rem` to `1.25rem` padding to retain compact, sculpted silhouettes.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system is conveyed through physical atmospheric layering, translucency, and frosted glass, avoiding artificial drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` and `#F8F9FA` flat backgrounds or cinematic full-bleed photographic canvasing.
- **Level 1 (Docked Header & Floating Badges):** Ultra-high surface blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), milky white opacity (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` on light; `rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.72)` on hero photography), wrapped in a 1px perimeter border (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`).
- **Level 2 (Floating Preview Docks & Interactive Cards):** Frosted glass surfaces with diffuse ambient lighting (`box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)`). Borders feature top-lit directional gradient hairlines mimicking glass bevels.
- **Overlay Tiers:** Dark hero backdrops use a subtle radial vignette gradient (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(0,0,0,0.45) 0%, rgba(0,0,0,0.2) 40%, rgba(0,0,0,0.65) 100%)`) to guarantee high contrast for display copy and navigation docks.

## Shapes

The design system uses a deliberate duality: **hyper-rounded pill forms** for interactions and navigation, contrasted with **sculptural softened rectangles** for architectural imagery.

### Shape Hierarchy
- **Pill Primitives (`rounded-full` / `9999px`):** Reserved for primary navigation bars, interactive buttons, pill tags, rating pills, and floating action triggers.
- **Architectural Frames (`rounded-2xl` / `1rem` to `1.5rem`):** Used on hero preview cards, horizontal ongoing project cards, and structural imagery. Curvature softens large photography without losing geometric architectural integrity.
- **Input Fields & Sliders (`rounded-full`):** Form inputs and search bars adopt full pill curvature to harmonize with the primary CTA buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Accent CTA:** Full pill (`rounded-full`), solid chartreuse fill (`#D8F235`), dark obsidian text (`#111111`), font weight `600`, horizontal padding `2rem`, vertical padding `0.875rem`. Hover state introduces subtle scale (`scale-105`) and slight luminance shift (`#E2FC3F`).
- **Secondary Dark CTA:** Full pill (`rounded-full`), deep black fill (`#1A1A1A`), crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), horizontal padding `1.75rem`, vertical padding `0.75rem`. Hover state features smooth opacity reduction (`opacity-90`) or slight elevation.
- **Ghost / Glass CTA:** Full pill, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` background with backdrop blur, 1px perimeter border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`, white text.

### Navigation Header
- **Floating Island Dock:** Detached from viewport edges, floating centered with `1.25rem` top margin. Full pill geometry (`rounded-full`), milky frosted backdrop (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`), 1px border (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`).
- **Navigation Links:** Neutral typography (`#111111`), medium weight (`500`), subtle hover transitions with no underlines. Contact metadata rendered in muted slate (`#666666`).

### Property Cards & Preview Trays
- **Hero Dock Cards (e.g., "Our Best 3 Deals For You"):** Horizontal frosted container with rounded corners (`rounded-2xl`). Houses a row of compact preview tiles with `rounded-xl` image masks, subtle dark scrim, and small, high-legibility typographic badges pinned to the bottom.
- **Ongoing Project Grid Cards:** Wide aspect ratio (`4:5` or `16:10`), rounded corners (`1.25rem`), minimal border, full bleed photography. Text labels sit outside below the image with bold architectural names and subtle location subtext.

### Ratings & Metric Highlights
- **Hero Social Proof Unit:** Asymmetrical presentation. Primary metric in large numerical weight (`3.5rem` font size, `-0.04em` tracking), followed by an inline denominator (`/5.0`) in slate `#777777`. Supporting micro-copy placed directly underneath in regular body-sm.

### Inputs & Controls
- **Search & Filter Bars:** Pill containers (`rounded-full`) with left-aligned icons, clean sans-serif placeholder text (`#777777`), and an integrated nested circular/pill submit button on the right edge.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimalist circular indicators with `#111111` or `#D8F235` fill upon selection, paired with hairline borders.
